Visibility and Limits

  • Agent1 sees the original source and untranslated target contracts, not generated translation. On adapt routes, requirement-only designs omit source commands, source result files and source test files.
  • Agent2 sees the translated target and the frozen handoff. It must preserve cases, inputs and expectations. For reference/reject routes, it receives no source directory or source handoff.
  • Source Observation requires actual Host replay. A Requirement-Derived Expectation is frozen before target execution and may intentionally differ from source behavior. Unresolved required expectations block completion.
  • Each active side can receive at most one additional artifact/harness repair session after an eligible rejection, within the original deadline. Integrity violations, timeouts and genuine behavioral differences do not trigger repair. Business implementation repair is outside this verifier.
  • Upstream Analyzer/Translator parallel orchestration is not integrated. The fixture E2E calls prepareTests, applies a fixed translation patch, then calls verifyTranslation. No live Analyzer or Translator runs in that experiment.
  • Service/CLI preparation capsules require Host-issued provenance. Local command policies and hash checks are not OS isolation or rollback. Agreement is evidence for the tested cases, not proof of business correctness.
