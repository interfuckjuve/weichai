ForeXplore: Black-Box Verification

multi-agent-black-box@1.0.0: Agent1 authors target tests before seeing the generated translation. Agent2 runs only after an eligible target failure, for diagnosis and at most one harness repair.

Visibility and Limits

  • Agent1 cannot access the generated translation or its explanation and cannot execute target tests during preparation. Source experiments are not original Source Observations; source-based expectations require replay against the unchanged original source.
  • Agent2 may inspect the translation after a failure. Only initial test design is translation-implementation black-box; the complete workflow is not strictly black-box.
  • Frozen cases, inputs, expectations, command manifests and original implementation files remain protected. Agent2 cannot repair business code, weaken expectations or hide failures. Integrity violations and timeouts do not enter the repair branch.
  • The reference/reject route is target-only verification, not black-box preparation coverage. Required unresolved expectations stop verification.
  • Upstream Analyzer/Translator parallel orchestration is not integrated. The fixture E2E uses a supplied analysis report and fixed translation patch. There is no automatic retranslation loop in this verifier.
  • Service/CLI preparation capsules require Host-issued provenance. Local command policies and hash checks are not OS isolation or rollback. Passing tests do not prove business correctness or test adequacy.
